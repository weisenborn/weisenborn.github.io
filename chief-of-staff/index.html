Chief of Staff

Chief of Staff is a private, personal productivity tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and prepares a daily summary email for that one person: the day's calendar, things to prepare for, and messages waiting on a reply.

It is not a public product or service. It has a single user, its owner, and is not available for anyone else to sign up for or use.

The app connects to its owner's Gmail account to read recent messages and to send the daily summary to the owner's own address. It never sends email to anyone else.
