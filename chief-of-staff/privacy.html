Chief of Staff: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Chief of Staff is a private tool with one user: its owner, who built it for personal use. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

What it accesses

With its owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail account to:

  • read recent email, to find items that need action or preparation;
  • send a daily summary email to the owner's own address.

It does not send email to any other recipient.

Where data is kept

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer, on an encrypted disk. There is no server and no cloud database. Sign-in tokens are kept in the computer's secure keychain.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Short excerpts of the owner's own messages are sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) solely to prepare the owner's own summary. Google user data is not used for advertising and is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account's security settings, and can delete all locally stored data.
